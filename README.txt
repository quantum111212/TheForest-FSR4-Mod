====================================================================
  The Forest - AMD FSR 4 Mod (v1.0) - 1-Click Installer
====================================================================

1-CLICK INSTALLATION:
  1. Extract this ZIP anywhere.
  2. Double-click "Install_FSR4.bat".
     - Auto-detects your Steam installation of The Forest.
     - Automatically downloads official BepInEx 5 & OptiScaler
       from GitHub and configures FSR 4 for your GPU.
  3. Launch The Forest normally!

1-CLICK UNINSTALL:
  Double-click "Uninstall_FSR4.bat" to remove all mod files and
  restore a clean vanilla installation.

IN-GAME HOTKEYS:
  F9     - Toggle FSR 4 ON / OFF live
  F10    - Cycle Quality Preset (NativeAA / UltraQuality / Quality /
           Balanced / Performance / UltraPerformance)
  Insert - Open OptiScaler overlay
